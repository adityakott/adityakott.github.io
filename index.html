Annual Cycle of the Intertropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ)

The animation illustrates the annual evolution of precipitation and 850 hPa winds, highlighting the seasonal migration of the Intertropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ). The P-E = 0 contours delineate the approximate boundaries of the ITCZ, enclosing the region of maximum tropical precipitation. Together, these fields provide a view of the seasonal evolution of tropical rainfall and its associated low-level circulation.

01 / ABOUT

About me.

Atmospheric scientist interested in tropical convection, large-scale circulations and fluid dynamics.

I hold a PhD from the Indian Institute of Science and am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. My research interests lie in tropical convection and its interactions with large-scale circulations. I am particularly interested in understanding how these interactions evolve in a warming climate.

My background in fluid dynamics spans a wide range of scales, from aircraft-scale flows to planetary-scale atmospheric dynamics. I also have a strong interest in computational fluid dynamics (CFD), with a particular focus on finite volume methods.
