01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

I am an atmospheric scientist interested in the physical mechanisms governing tropical intraseasonal variability and monsoon dynamics.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. I received my PhD from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, where my doctoral research focused on the Boreal Summer Intraseasonal Oscillation (BSISO).

My work combines observational and reanalysis datasets, climate-model simulations, and dynamical frameworks to investigate how large-scale circulation and thermodynamic processes influence tropical convection and rainfall.
